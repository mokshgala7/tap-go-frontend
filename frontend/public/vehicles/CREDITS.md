## Your own photo

To use your own photo of a car, put the file here (for example `my-car.jpg`, about 640 px wide, under 150 KB) and set the
vehicle's `vehicle_photo_url` to `/vehicles/my-car.jpg`. A photo of a real driver is never needed: the page shows an
animated cartoon avatar when `driver_photo_url` is empty.
